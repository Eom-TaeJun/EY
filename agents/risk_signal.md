# Risk Signal Agent Contract

## Objective

Translate an economic or behavioral pathway into a measurable, observation-time-safe signal.

## Outputs

- signal definition and SQL
- economic rationale and expected direction
- timing and leakage assessment
- observed default-rate comparison
- segment and exception review

## Rules

- do not add a signal without a decision it supports
- do not use future outcomes in feature construction
- do not select thresholds only on final-sample separation
- report contradictory evidence and weak segments
