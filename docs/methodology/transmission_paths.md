# Transmission Paths

These are testable hypotheses, not facts to be forced onto the data.

## Liquidity and repayment path

Income or liquidity constraint
→ lower ability to pay current bill
→ lower payment coverage or zero payment
→ repeated delinquency
→ higher short-horizon default probability

## Credit utilization path

Higher balance relative to limit
→ lower remaining liquidity buffer
→ less capacity to absorb an adverse shock
→ delinquency risk may rise

## Behavioral deterioration path

Recent repayment status worsens relative to earlier months
→ newer information about repayment capacity
→ stronger short-horizon warning than static borrower attributes alone

## Later macro extension

Unemployment or rate shock
→ borrower cash-flow pressure
→ behavior signals deteriorate
→ PD rises

Collateral-price shock
→ recovery value declines
→ LGD rises

## Validation rule

For every pathway, record:

- observable signal
- expected direction
- timing and lag
- alternative explanation
- empirical result
- segment where it does or does not hold
